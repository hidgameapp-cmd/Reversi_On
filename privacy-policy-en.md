# Privacy Policy

**App: Reversi-On**

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Hideki Games ("we", "us") provides the iOS application "Reversi-On" (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the App.

*This is a courtesy translation. The Japanese version is the authoritative text; in case of any discrepancy, the Japanese version prevails.*

---

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information**

- A user identifier (UID) generated automatically when you first launch the App. This is a random string and does not by itself identify you.
- A display name that you may set. A default name is generated automatically.
- An identifier associated with your Apple ID, if you choose to link one via Sign in with Apple. **We do not request or receive your name or email address.**

**Gameplay information**

Game results (win/loss and disc counts), move records, rating, timestamps, and the identifier of your opponent.

**Device and usage information**

Device model, OS version, app version, language setting, crash reports, and feature usage statistics.

**Information transmitted automatically**

IP address and access timestamps, recorded in server logs as an inherent part of network communication.

**What we do not collect**

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, location, contacts, photos, calendar, microphone or camera data, or the advertising identifier (IDFA). The App displays no advertising and performs no tracking.

---

## 2. How we use information

1. To operate the App and enable gameplay
2. To match you with an opponent
3. To calculate and display ratings and leaderboards
4. To detect and prevent cheating, such as modified clients or rating manipulation
5. To investigate defects and improve quality
6. To respond to your inquiries

---

## 3. Disclosure to third parties

We do not disclose information to third parties except with your consent, where required by law, or where necessary to protect life, body or property and obtaining consent is difficult.

**We never sell, rent, or share information for advertising purposes.**

Your display name and rating are shown to your opponent. This is a function of the App rather than a disclosure to a third party.

---

## 4. Processors and international transfers

The App uses Firebase, operated by **Google LLC (United States)**, for authentication, database, and crash reporting. We therefore entrust the handling of the information described in Section 1 to Google LLC.

As required by Article 28 of Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information:

| | |
|---|---|
| Recipient | Google LLC |
| Country | United States of America |
| Data protection regime | The United States has no comprehensive federal data protection statute; protection is provided through sector-specific federal laws and state laws. |
| Safeguards | Google LLC maintains a framework, based on data processing terms and standard contractual clauses, for continuously implementing measures equivalent to those required of a personal information handling business operator. |

Most of your data (match results, ratings, and account information) is stored in the **asia-northeast1 (Tokyo)** region. However, temporary connection-status information (used only to detect whether you are online or offline during a match) is stored in the **asia-southeast1 (Singapore)** region, as the Tokyo region is not available for this specific service. This information does not include your name or match content. Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy

For further details on the safeguards in place, please contact us at the address given at the end of this policy.

---

## 5. Security measures

- All communication uses encrypted (TLS) channels
- Database access rules restrict each user to reading only data relevant to them
- Game results and ratings cannot be written directly from user devices; they are updated only on the server
- Development and production environments are separated, with restricted access to production

---

## 6. Retention and deletion

| Information | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account, rating, record | Until the account is deleted |
| Move records | 90 days after the game ends |
| Crash and usage data | 14 months (Firebase default) |
| Server access logs | Per Google's retention schedule |

When you delete your account, your account information and associated game data are deleted. We may continue to retain **aggregated, non-identifiable statistics** to the extent necessary for leaderboard integrity.

---

## 7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen in the App. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

---

## 8. Your rights

You may request notification of purpose of use, disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, erasure, or suspension of provision to third parties, in respect of your personal data.

To make a request, contact us at the address given at the end of this policy, including the user identifier (UID) shown on the App's Settings screen. We will respond without delay once we have verified that the request comes from you.

Because we do not collect email addresses, **the UID is the only means of verifying your identity.** After the App is deleted we may be unable to verify a request.

---

## 9. Children's privacy

The App is rated for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The information the App collects is limited to the minimum necessary to provide gameplay. Parents or guardians who wish to have information deleted may contact us at the address given at the end of this policy.

---

## 10. Advertising and tracking

The App displays no advertising and performs no tracking. No App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt is shown.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy in response to changes in law or to the App's features. We will give advance notice of material changes within the App or on its App Store page.
